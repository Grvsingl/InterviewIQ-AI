Is InterviewIQ AI free? −

Yes! InterviewIQ AI is completely free during our early access phase. No credit card required. We'll be introducing InterviewIQ Pro in the future with additional premium features.

How do AI mock interviews work? +

You answer questions on video or voice, and our AI provides real-time feedback on content, clarity, structure, and delivery. It's like having a personal interview coach.

Do I need a webcam? +

For video interviews, yes. But we also support voice-only practice if you prefer to start without video.

Can I upload my resume? +

Absolutely. Upload your resume and we'll analyze it for ATS compatibility, content strength, and improvement opportunities.

Which interview types are supported? +

We support HR, technical, behavioral, company-specific, voice, video, and coding interviews.

How accurate is the ATS analysis? +

Our ATS analysis uses industry-standard algorithms to match your resume against job descriptions, with a high degree of accuracy.

Does it support technical interviews? +

Yes, we have a dedicated coding interview module with practice problems and AI-generated hints.

Is my data secure? +

Absolutely. We use enterprise-grade encryption and never share your data with third parties.

Will more AI features be added? +

Yes! We're constantly improving and adding new features. Follow us for updates.

What is InterviewIQ Pro? +

InterviewIQ Pro is our upcoming premium tier with advanced analytics, unlimited practice, and personalized coaching sessions.
